Runes

League Classic's rune book. A page is 9 marks, 9 seals, 9 glyphs and 3 quintessences, bought with IP and slotted before the game starts.

Note Minor runes put an off-slot stat into a slot — health on a mark, say. They give less than the slot's native stat and cost more (650 IP against 500).
